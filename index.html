Cancer information chatbot

In development

An AI assistant that explains cancer topics to patients and families in plain language.

+

The problem

After a diagnosis, people search online and land on pages that are either too technical or not trustworthy. They need clear answers, fast, at any hour.

What we’re building

A conversational assistant, built by a small team, that answers questions about cancer types, tests, terms and treatment options using vetted medical sources.

Safety by design

It gives general information and points people to their care team. It doesn’t diagnose or recommend treatment.

Traffic monitoring system

Prototype

Detects, tracks and counts vehicles in video, running on a laptop with no extra hardware.

+

The problem

Counting traffic by hand is slow. Cameras already exist; the missing piece is software that turns footage into numbers.

What I’m building

A pipeline that detects vehicles in each frame, follows them across frames, and counts each one once as it crosses a line.

What I’m learning

Campus travel buddy

In design

A website where college students book trips and find peers from their campus on the same bus, train or flight.

+

The problem

Students travel home the same weekends, often alone, without knowing a classmate is on the same bus.

How it works

Pick your college, book bus, train or flight tickets, and see other students from your college on the same journey. You choose whether you’re visible, with or without a booking.

Trust

Two-factor sign-in using a student ID so only real students from the college can see each other.

Web appAuthenticationPrivacy controls

Vehicle-fit navigation

Concept

GPS routing that checks whether your vehicle fits the road and follows local traffic rules.

+

The problem

Navigation apps pick the fastest route, not one a bus or truck can actually take. Road width and rules differ by country.

The idea

Combine road-width data and country-specific regulations with the vehicle’s dimensions, and only suggest routes it can pass through.
